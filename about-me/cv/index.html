Academic Profile

Clinical Associate Professor of Psychiatry at SUNY Upstate Medical University with sustained contributions in medical student, psychiatry resident, family medicine resident, and addiction fellow teaching; addiction and pain psychiatry clinical education; mentorship; professional service; and indexed educational scholarship. Editor-in-Chief of the Psychiatry & Addiction Section for StatPearls Publishing, with multiple NCBI Bookshelf / PubMed-indexed educational publications across psychiatry, addiction, suicide risk, child psychiatry, controlled-substance prescribing, and behavioral health.

He leads PARS — the Psychiatry Assessment and Recommendations Service at Upstate Psychiatry — a single-appointment consultation and intake program, and serves as an AI Clinical Lead for Psychiatry for Meta’s medical AI work (via Tundra), applying clinical psychiatric expertise to mental health and substance-use AI systems.
