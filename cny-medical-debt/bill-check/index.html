Medical Bill Check

Free tool · works in all 50 states and Washington, DC · Updated October 2026

Private by design. Everything you enter stays in your browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere, stored, or seen by anyone. Do not enter your name, birth date, or any account or bill numbers. This tool does not need them and does not want them.

Answer a few questions about a hospital bill you received, or one you are expecting. The tool checks your state’s hospital financial-aid rules, flags common billing problems, and gives you a plain-language plan for what to do next.

Think of this as a pre-screen before you call for help. Do the homework here in about two minutes, copy your summary at the end, and bring it when you call. It shortens the intake and gets you to answers faster.
