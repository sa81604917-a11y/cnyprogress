Free help with New York hospital bills: financial aid you may qualify for, your rights under state law, and where to get help in Central NY.

Need help with a medical bill?

Medical bills can be confusing, and many patients pay more than they actually owe. Depending on your income, insurance and the hospital involved, you may qualify for financial assistance, have rights related to collections, or have a billing or insurance issue that can be challenged.

You may be able to get help with:

  • Hospital financial assistance/charity care
  • Bills you cannot afford
  • Medical debt in collections
  • Insurance denials or billing problems
  • Understanding your rights under New York law
  • Finding legal or advocacy help when needed

New York patients have important protections

New York has expanded hospital financial-assistance requirements and protections against aggressive medical-debt collection. Depending on your income and circumstances, you may qualify for substantial discounts or complete forgiveness of hospital charges. New York’s Hospital Financial Aid Law requires hospitals to offer free or discounted care based on income. Below 200% of the federal poverty level (under $31,920 single / $66,000 family of four), the hospital must waive all charges. Up to 400% ($63,840 / $132,000) may qualify for discounts — including insured patients who exhausted benefits or spent over 10% of household income on medical costs last year.

About the CNY Medical Debt Project

This project is working to make medical billing and financial-assistance systems easier to understand, connect patients with existing help, track medical-debt practices in Central New York, and encourage hospitals and policymakers to improve access and accountability.
