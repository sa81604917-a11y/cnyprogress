I’m Sunny Aslam, an addiction psychiatrist and Clinical Associate Professor of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences at SUNY Upstate Medical University.

My work has included psychiatric and addiction care for people experiencing homelessness in Syracuse. Today, I also work in AI medical safety testing and explore how technology might help people navigate health information and public services.

A larger question runs through my work: how can we make systems easier to use, more trustworthy, and more responsive to people who are too often overlooked?

Making help easier to find

I’m now exploring a related question: could AI help people understand options for medical bills, benefits, housing, and other basic needs? Trust, privacy, and access to a human being are central to that exploration.

My work includes testing the boundaries of AI in medical contexts. I’m especially interested in how systems respond when conversations involve mental health or substance use.
