I’m Sunny Aslam, an addiction psychiatrist and Clinical Associate Professor of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences at SUNY Upstate Medical University.

My work has included psychiatric and addiction care for people experiencing homelessness in Syracuse. Today, I also work in AI medical safety testing and explore how technology might help people navigate health information and public services: whether a hospital bill qualifies for financial aid, where to find addiction treatment nearby, what benefits a family might be missing.

A larger question runs through my work: how can we make systems easier to use, more trustworthy, and more responsive to people who are too often overlooked?
