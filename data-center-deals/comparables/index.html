Verification notes

Last verified: October 2026

  • West Feliciana figures verified October 2026: PILOT/lease via the parish Industrial Development Board; parish officials project up to ~$90M/year public revenue on 330 MW utility capacity (projected, not realized); $16M in wells and mains deeded to the parish. Note: in 2026 an industry-backed proposal before the Louisiana Tax Commission sought to sharply reduce data-center equipment valuations, which would have cut the projected revenue roughly in half (Bloomberg, October 2026) — the tax-treatment assumptions behind any headline number must be stated explicitly.
  • STAMP figures verified October 2026: ~$1.4B requested sales-tax exemption plus ~$31M mortgage-tax exemption; $285M PILOT over 30 years (~$9.5M/year); ~125 permanent jobs. (Stream says the sales-tax figure is a failsafe if the state’s internet-data-center exemption does not apply.)
  • New York framework verified October 2026: at least $1 million in community investment per MW of utility demand (voluntary guidance; flexible upfront-or-lifetime target).

MW means maximum contracted utility demand unless stated otherwise. Last updated: October 2026. Proposed negotiating benchmarks, not legal advice; particular terms depend on municipal authority, utility regulation and project circumstances.
