Data Center Deal Comparables

Selected U.S. benchmarks for host-community negotiations

How real U.S. data-center deals compare
Deal MW basis Taxes / PILOT Community value and timing Power and infrastructure Water Neighbor protections Transparency
DeForest, WI — QTS (negotiated terms; project collapsed) Not publicly disclosed ($12B campus, up to 1,600 acres) Contractual minimum taxable-value floor $50M community fund (education, housing, workforce) Developer pays 100% of project grid infrastructure; 750 MW new renewables Private-well guarantees, quarter-mile setback Buyout at 110% of assessed value for materially affected homes Secret process; deal died when terms surfaced
West Feliciana Parish, LA — Hut 8 (executed agreement) 330 MW contracted utility demand PILOT/lease structure via parish Industrial Development Board (not full taxation); developer-donated property leased back ~$90M/yr projected PILOT revenue (about $273,000 per MW of contracted utility demand per year); property-tax credits to households authorized by state law Developer-funded substation and generation; ratepayer protections Closed-loop recycling, no aquifer impact; $16M wells and mains deeded to public No standalone terms publicly documented No NDA; parish publicly pitches the deal as the model
STAMP campus, Genesee County, NY — Stream (proposal stage) 500 MW reported demand Requested exemptions per GCEDC’s March 2026 cost-benefit analysis (Stream US Data Centers LLC): about $1.43B in sales-tax exemptions (roughly $716M local plus roughly $716M state) and about $31.2M in mortgage-tax exemptions (roughly $15.6M local plus roughly $15.6M state); $285M PILOT over 30 years. (Stream says the sales-tax request is a failsafe if the state internet-data-center exemption does not apply.) ~$9.5M/yr PILOT and host payments over 30 years (about $19,000/MW/yr); ~125 permanent jobs Not publicly detailed Not publicly detailed Not publicly detailed The cautionary case for negotiating late
New York State Host Community Investment Framework (voluntary guidance, Sept 2026) Per MW of utility demand Recommends at least $1M in community investment per MW Benchmark, not a payment; flexible upfront-or-lifetime investment target negotiated locally (not necessarily cash to the host community) Guidance only; terms set locally — — —

Verification notes

Last verified: October 2026

  • West Feliciana figures verified October 2026: PILOT/lease via the parish Industrial Development Board; up to ~$90M/year projected public revenue on 330 MW utility capacity; $16M in wells and mains deeded to the parish.
  • STAMP figures verified October 2026: ~$1.4B requested sales-tax exemption plus ~$31M mortgage-tax exemption; $285M PILOT over 30 years (~$9.5M/year); ~125 permanent jobs. (Stream says the sales-tax figure is a failsafe if the state’s internet-data-center exemption does not apply.)
  • New York framework verified October 2026: $1M/MW community-investment benchmark, voluntary guidance.

MW means maximum contracted utility demand unless stated otherwise. Last updated: October 2026. Proposed negotiating benchmarks, not legal advice; particular terms depend on municipal authority, utility regulation and project circumstances.
