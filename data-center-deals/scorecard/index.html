Check a real data center proposal against the 30-point model host-community term sheet. Each term below is a protection the public side should demand; check the ones the proposal actually meets. Your tally updates as you go.

How to score honestly. One guiding principle runs through all 30 terms: costs caused by the project are not community benefits. Mitigation keeps the public from losing money and buys the developer nothing extra; community value is negotiated separately.

Count a term only when the proposal commits to it in writing. A promise in a press release or a public hearing is not a contract term.
