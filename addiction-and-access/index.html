Addiction and Access

Compassionate care. Fewer barriers. Respect for every person.

As an addiction psychiatrist, I care about both what happens in treatment and what makes treatment possible. My approach centers on understanding each person’s circumstances, supporting their goals, and treating them with dignity.

Access means more than having a service available. It means being able to find care, afford it, reach it, and feel welcome when you arrive.
